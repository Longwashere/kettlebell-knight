## Sunken Cistern — The Abyssal Sluices
**`sunken_cistern/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE WORK: THE LIVING WATERWAYS**
Vast vaulted dwarven cistern halls carved from mountain rock, ribbed stone arches, gleaming bronze sluice gates
and great gears channeling crystal-clear turquoise mountain water through carved aqueducts and waterfalls. Dwarven
stonemasons with lanterns work on scaffolds; warm golden lantern light shimmers on pure water. Serene, majestic, orderly.

**`sunken_cistern/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE FLOOD: RIFT FROM THE TRENCH**
The same cistern drowned: the lowest floodgate shattered open onto a black oceanic abyss, corrosive black brine
surging up through broken bronze sluices. Armored dredge-crabs (giant crabs) climb the wreckage, a hulking sewer
lurker and green slimes in the murk, bioluminescent cyan horrors glowing beneath the water, plague rats on floating
debris, a drowned overseer's lantern sinking. Murky teal and black palette, sickly glow.

## Cathedral Crypt — The Osseous Sepulchers
**`cathedral_crypt/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE HALLOWED: TOMBS OF THE SAINTS**
A holy catacomb of white alabaster sepulchers in candlelit gothic vaults. Stone effigies of knights lie on their tombs,
iron weapons laid upon their chests; glowing golden prayer-ward runes seal each tomb; hooded clerics chant with
censers; warm gold candlelight and incense haze. Peaceful, sacred.

**`cathedral_crypt/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — DESECRATION: THE BROKEN SEALS**
The same crypt in necromantic winter: frost-rimed shattered alabaster seals, a gaunt exiled lich archmage in black
robes holding a black grimoire wreathed in pale blue frost-fire. Skeleton warriors with rusted broadswords and a
skeleton knight claw out of broken tombs, skeleton archers in the shadows, a hulking bone golem behind, crypt crawlers
and bats on the ceiling. Icy blue and bone-grey palette with ghostly soul wisps.

## Blood Cemetery — The Weeping Mausoleums
**`blood_cemetery/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE SERENITY: CLOUD AND PETAL**
A serene clifftop rose-garden cemetery on terraced hillsides under a gentle golden autumn sky with soft clouds.
Red roses bloom over neat family graves and white headstones; mourners lay fresh roses; a small stone chapel with a
bell tower above. Dignified, peaceful, warm amber and rose palette, falling autumn leaves and petals.

**`blood_cemetery/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE BLEEDING: THE CRIMSON MOON**
The same cemetery under a blood-red eclipse: a huge moon with a weeping wound dripping scarlet light, the sky like
coagulated blood. The ground has liquefied into crimson mire; coffins burst through shattered headstones; a towering
Grave Colossus of fused rotting corpses rises in the distance. Wraiths and ghosts drift, lantern wisps, storm crows and
bats circle, a wolf howls on a broken tomb. Deep crimson and black palette.

## Belfry Ramparts — The Tempest Spires
**`belfry_ramparts/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE TOWER: THE VOICE OF THE REALM**
A soaring citadel bell tower high above the clouds at golden dawn: the Great Carillon of gleaming brass and electrum
bells ringing, visible sound rings radiating outward. Proud battlements with banners, knights standing guard on the
ramparts, sea of clouds below, radiant sunlight. Heroic, majestic, unassailable.

**`belfry_ramparts/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE SILENCE: THE BELLS CAST DOWN**
The same towers swallowed by an eternal storm: lightning, howling rain, giant brass bells tumbling from broken mounts
and shattering on the rocks far below. Stone gargoyles break from their perches with glowing eyes, harpies and storm
crows ride the winds, a skeleton archer on the crumbling parapet. Storm-blue and slate-grey palette with white lightning.

## Cathedral Vault — The Gilded Treasury
**`cathedral_vault/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE SANCTUARY: THE TREASURE OF SAINTS**
A gleaming golden reliquary vault: golden chests of twelve dynasties, covenant scrolls, star-metal ingots, holy
artifacts on altars. Noble brass clockwork guardian knights with glowing solar crystals in their chests stand silent
watch; master clocksmiths tune one; warm sunbeams through high windows. Rich gold, ivory and warm light.

**`cathedral_vault/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE PARANOIA: THE LOCKED TOMB**
The same vault sealed and dark: massive vault doors welded shut with dripping molten lead, a mad artificer hunched
over sparking automaton cores, whispers of purple void-mist. Clockwork sentinels turned into razor-geared killers,
grinding gears and bone powder on golden floors, a stone golem, a gargoyle, vault hounds, a dark mage in the shadows.
Tarnished gold, cold iron and sickly violet palette.

## Volcanic Forge — The Molten Caldera
**`volcanic_forge/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE FORGE: WHERE BELLS WERE BORN**
A grand subterranean dwarven foundry in the roots of the world: master smiths hammering a huge glowing bell on an
anvil, molten star-fire metal pouring from crucibles into bell moulds, a fallen meteorite being worked, steam from a
quench. Orange forge light on stone pillars, sparks flying. Sacred, mighty, industrious.

**`volcanic_forge/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE CATACLYSM: THE CALDERA BURSTS**
The same foundry submerged in rivers of magma: the caldera has burst, boiling basalt flooding the workshops, half-
melted anvils and bell moulds, cinder silhouettes of burned smiths. Cinder imps and hellhounds dance in the ashes,
ember serpents slither through lava, a vast magma dragon's silhouette in the smoke beyond. Red, black and orange.

## Eldritch Altar — The Sacrificial Void Rift
**`eldritch_altar/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE OBSERVATORY: THE HARMONY OF STARS**
A high astral observatory temple under a clear starry night: silver telescopes, a giant golden astrolabe, orderly
glowing constellations, robed monks chanting celestial hymns in a circle around a radiant altar. Deep blue, silver and
gold palette, calm and enlightened.

**`eldritch_altar/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE TEAR: THE EYES IN THE DARK**
The same observatory torn apart: the golden astrolabe shattered, a jagged rift ripped across the sky leaking impossible
non-euclidean geometry and countless staring eyes. Mad high priests in tattered robes at a blood-stained sacrificial
altar, a demon archbishop and void prophet presiding, wraiths and ghosts, a nightmare stag in the gloom. The temple floats
in cosmic void. Violet, magenta and black palette.

## Obsidian Throne — The Pinnacle of Despair
**`obsidian_throne/chapter1_glory.jpg` — THE PINNACLE: SEAT OF THE SUN**
The Sun King on a polished white marble throne atop the summit of the world, golden sun standards and banners, a
radiant throne room open to the sky, loyal knights and people kneeling, the king shouldering a great iron burden with
dignity. White marble, gold and sunlight. Righteous, prosperous.

**`obsidian_throne/chapter2_ruin.jpg` — THE BETRAYAL: THE UNBURDENING**
The same throne room corrupted: a cowardly last emperor slumped on a throne turning to black obsidian, shaking hands
with a shadowy void entity; his knights turning into obsidian glass statues; withered banners, ash drifting, the
kingdom outside atrophied and grey. Throne guardians, gargoyles and hellhounds in the shadows. Black, ash-grey and
cold purple palette.
